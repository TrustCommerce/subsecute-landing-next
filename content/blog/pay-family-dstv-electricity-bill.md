You do not need access to anyone's bank account to pay their bill. You need one number.

## What you actually need

**For DSTV or GOtv:** the smartcard or IUC number, and the package they are on.

**For electricity:** the meter number and the disco. Eko, Ikeja, Abuja, Enugu, Ibadan and so on. Also whether the meter is prepaid or postpaid, because prepaid gives you a token to send them and postpaid clears a balance.

**For internet or water:** the account number on the bill.

Get these once, save them somewhere, and you never have to ask again. That is the whole trick. Most of the friction in this is not the payment. It is the WhatsApp exchange every month asking for a number you already had.

## How the payment works

Any Nigerian bill payment platform takes the identifier, confirms the name attached to it, and shows you the amount. You confirm and pay. For prepaid electricity you get a token, which you send to whoever is at the house to enter into the meter.

That name confirmation step matters. It is your check that you are paying the right meter, since a wrong digit will happily pay a stranger's bill.

## The number you keep asking for

A familiar exchange. You open WhatsApp on a Sunday evening to buy your mother units. You need the meter number. You scroll up to find where she sent it last time, give up after a minute, and type "mummy send me the meter number again".

She sends a photo of the meter. You squint at it. You buy the units. You send the token. Two of the digits are wrong and you do the whole thing again.

Next month, same Sunday, same message.

The payment took forty seconds. Everything around it took twenty minutes.

## Making it stop being a monthly task

Doing this by hand every month works. It is just twelve small errands a year, plus the ones you forget, plus the call you get when the light goes off.

Set it up once in Subsecute instead. The bill details are saved, the payment runs on schedule, and you get a reminder before each one so you can skip a month if you need to. If it is a prepaid meter, the token goes where it needs to go without you copying anything.

The point is not that it saves five minutes. It is that you stop carrying the date in your head.
