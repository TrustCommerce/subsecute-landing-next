Netflix and Spotify bill in naira in Nigeria. ChatGPT Plus does not. Neither does Claude, Twitch, Midjourney, or most of the tools you actually pay a foreign company for.

They bill in US dollars. That single fact causes most of the trouble Nigerians have with them, and it is the same trouble every time, whichever service it is.

## What "billed in dollars" actually costs you

Take ChatGPT Plus at $20 a month. Your bank converts that $20 at its own rate on the day the charge lands, then usually adds a small cross-border fee. So the naira figure moves month to month even though the dollar price never changes.

Two things follow from that. Your budget is a moving target. And your international spending limit matters, because $20 in dollars eats a real chunk of a low monthly cap once the naira rate is applied.

Every dollar subscription behaves this way. The number on the invoice is fixed in dollars and unpredictable in naira.

## How to subscribe (the flow is the same everywhere)

Using ChatGPT Plus as the example:

1. Log in to [chatgpt.com](https://chatgpt.com).
2. Open Settings, then Subscription, and choose Plus.
3. Enter your card details.
4. Use a billing address that matches your card.

Before you start, open your bank app and confirm two toggles are on: online or e-commerce transactions, and international transactions. A dollar charge needs the international one, and some banks reset it quietly after a card replacement.

## The same problem hits Claude, Twitch and the rest

Different service, identical mechanics. Here is what you are dealing with for the ones Nigerians ask about most.

### ChatGPT Plus

$20 a month, billed by OpenAI in dollars. Subscribe at [chatgpt.com](https://chatgpt.com). The most common failure is a monthly international limit that does not have room for $20 at the current rate.

### Claude Pro

Anthropic's Claude Pro is around $20 a month, also billed in dollars. You subscribe inside [claude.ai](https://claude.ai) under your plan settings. Paying for Claude in Nigeria works exactly like ChatGPT: a naira card with international transactions enabled, or a dollar card funded ahead of the date. Same dollar charge, same reasons it fails.

### Twitch

Twitch subscriptions to a channel are usually a few dollars a month each, billed in dollars through [twitch.tv](https://www.twitch.tv). If you support more than one streamer, several small dollar charges land on different dates, which is harder to track than one big one.

### Other dollar subscriptions

Midjourney, Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Notion, Figma and most creator and developer tools bill in dollars too. Watch for one trap: anything you buy through your Apple or Google account shows up as a single charge from Apple or Google, not from the app, so it hides in your statement even though it is still a dollar subscription underneath.

## Why these fail for Nigerians more than naira subscriptions

Because it is a dollar charge:

- Your bank's monthly international limit has to cover the amount at whatever the rate is that day
- If the naira moves, a charge that fit last month might not fit this month
- International transactions must be enabled, and some banks reset that quietly
- Any conversion fee is on top, so budget slightly above the dollar amount

Nobody tells you when the charge fails. With ChatGPT or Claude you find out when the model gets slower or you hit a limit mid task. With Twitch you find out when your sub badge disappears.

## The practical approach

Keep a buffer. Whatever the dollar figure costs today, plan for a bit more, because the rate will not wait for you.
