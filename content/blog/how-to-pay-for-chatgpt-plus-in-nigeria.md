Netflix and Spotify bill in naira in Nigeria. ChatGPT Plus does not. Neither does Claude, Midjourney, or most of the tools you pay a foreign company for.

They bill abroad, usually in US dollars. That single fact causes most of the trouble Nigerians have with them, and it is the same trouble every time, whichever service it is.

## What a foreign charge actually costs you

Take ChatGPT Plus at $20 a month. Your bank converts that $20 to naira at its own rate on the day the charge lands, and can add a cross-border fee on top. So the naira figure moves month to month even though the dollar price never changes.

Two things follow from that. Your budget is a moving target. And your card's international spending limit matters, because a $20 charge has to fit under whatever your bank currently allows for foreign transactions.

## How to subscribe (the flow is the same everywhere)

Using ChatGPT Plus as the example:

1. Log in to [chatgpt.com](https://chatgpt.com).
2. Open Settings, then Subscription, and choose Plus.
3. Enter your card details.
4. Use a billing address that matches your card.

Before you start, make sure international transactions are actually enabled on your card. Nigerian banks switched them off through the 2022 dollar shortage, and only brought them back in July 2025, when [GTBank, Stanbic IBTC, First Bank and Wema resumed the service](https://www.cnbcafrica.com/2025/nigerian-banks-resume-naira-debit-card-use-abroad-after-three-year-pause) with limits as low as $500 a month.

Those limits have risen sharply since. As of [September 2026](https://nairametrics.com/2026/09/07/gtco-firstbank-zenith-bank-others-raise-dollar-spending-limits-as-fx-scarcity-eases/), GTBank allows $40,000 a quarter, Zenith $50,000 a year, UBA $20,000 on POS, FirstBank $10,000 a quarter and Stanbic IBTC $8,000, on the back of foreign reserves crossing $54 billion. For a $20 subscription the ceiling is no longer the problem. Whether the card is switched on for international use, and whether the charge clears on the day, still is.

## The same problem hits Claude, Twitch and the rest

Here is what you are actually dealing with for the ones Nigerians ask about most.

### ChatGPT Plus

$20 a month, billed by OpenAI in US dollars. Subscribe at [chatgpt.com](https://chatgpt.com).

### Claude Pro

$20 a month, or $17 a month paid annually, billed by Anthropic in US dollars. It works exactly like ChatGPT: a naira card with international transactions enabled, or a dollar card funded ahead of the date. [How to pay for Claude in Nigeria](/blog/how-to-pay-for-claude-in-nigeria) covers it on its own.

### Twitch

Twitch is the exception worth knowing about. It uses local pricing, so the amount is shown in your local currency and varies by country rather than being a flat dollar figure. A Tier 1 channel subscription is around $5.99 a month in the US. On the Twitch mobile apps it costs more, roughly $7.99, because Apple and Google add their own cut, and that charge goes through your App Store or Google account rather than Twitch directly.

### Other foreign subscriptions

Midjourney, Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Notion and Figma mostly bill in dollars too. Watch for one trap that applies to Twitch as well: anything you buy through your Apple or Google account shows up as a single charge from Apple or Google, not from the app, so it hides in your statement even though it is still a foreign subscription underneath.

## Why these fail for Nigerians more than naira subscriptions

Because the charge is foreign:

- International transactions have to be switched on for the card at all, which many banks only restored in July 2025
- If the naira moves, a charge that fit last month might not fit this month
- International transactions have to be enabled on your card in the first place
- Any conversion fee is on top, so budget slightly above the sticker price

Nobody warns you when a charge fails. You usually find out after the fact, when the subscription simply stops working.

## The practical approach

Keep a buffer. Whatever the charge costs today, plan for a bit more, because the rate will not wait for you.

Better still, stop holding the dates in your head. Subsecute tracks each renewal, reminds you before it lands, and charges your saved card or direct debit ahead of the date so the money is on the card before OpenAI asks for it. Whether it is ChatGPT, Claude or something else, you are not gambling on whether the charge clears on the exact day it is due.
