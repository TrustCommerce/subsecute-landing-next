ChatGPT Plus is different from Netflix and Spotify in one important way. It bills in dollars, not naira.

That single fact causes most of the trouble Nigerians have with it.

## What it actually costs you

Plus is $20 a month. Your bank converts that at its own rate on the day, then usually adds a small cross-border fee. So the naira figure moves month to month even though the dollar price never changes.

Two things follow from that. Your budget is a moving target. And your international spending limit matters, because $20 eats a real chunk of a low monthly cap.

## How to subscribe

1. Log in to chatgpt.com.
2. Open Settings, then Subscription, and choose Plus.
3. Enter your card details.
4. Use a billing address that matches your card.

## Why it fails for Nigerians more than other subscriptions

Because it is a dollar charge:

- Your bank's monthly international limit has to cover $20 at whatever the rate is that day
- If the naira moves, a charge that fit last month might not fit this month
- International transactions must be enabled, and some banks reset that quietly
- Any conversion fee is on top, so budget slightly above the dollar amount

Nobody tells you when the charge fails. You find out when the model gets slower or you hit a limit mid task.

## The practical approach

Keep a buffer. Whatever $20 costs today, plan for a bit more, because the rate will not wait for you.
