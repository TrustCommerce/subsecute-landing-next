Netflix bills in naira in Nigeria, so you are not converting anything. Where it gets messy is which card actually goes through, and what happens when the renewal date comes and your bank says no.

## What Netflix costs

Last confirmed pricing:

| Plan | Price per month | Screens |
|---|---|---|
| Mobile | ₦2,500 | 1 (phone or tablet) |
| Basic | ₦4,000 | 1 |
| Standard | ₦6,500 | 2 |
| Premium | ₦8,500 | 4 |

There is no annual plan in Nigeria. Everything is billed monthly, on the same date you first subscribed.

## The payment methods that work

**Naira debit card.** Verve, Visa or Mastercard from your bank. Netflix bills in naira, so a working naira card is usually enough. The catch is that Netflix is still a foreign merchant, so the charge can be blocked if your bank has international transactions switched off or capped. Many banks restricted this between 2022 and 2023 and only eased it in 2025, so if your card is being declined, that is the first thing to check.

**Virtual card.** Works the same way. Useful if you want the subscription separated from your main account.

**Someone else's card.** Netflix does not care whose card it is, as long as the details are right. This is how a lot of family plans get paid.

## Setting it up

1. Go to [netflix.com/ng](https://www.netflix.com/ng) on a browser. The app works too, but the browser is easier if you are switching plans.
2. Sign in or create an account.
3. Pick your plan.
4. Choose Credit or Debit Card.
5. Enter your card details and your real address.
6. Confirm the charge.

Netflix will bill you the same date every month until you cancel.

## Why it fails on renewal day

The first charge usually works. It is month three that catches people.

Common reasons:

- Your card's international transactions got switched off, or the charge ran into your bank's limit
- Your bank lowered its monthly international limit
- Your card expired and Netflix kept trying the old one
- The account simply did not have enough in it that morning

Netflix retries for a few days, then suspends the account. You find out when you sit down to watch something.
