Nigeria has some of the cheapest Spotify pricing in the world. The plans are billed in naira, so the money side is simple. Keeping it running is where people struggle.

## What it costs

| Plan | Price per month | Who it is for |
|---|---|---|
| Individual | ₦1,600 | One person |
| Duo | ₦2,100 | Two people at the same address |
| Family | ₦2,500 | Up to six people at the same address |
| Student | ₦800 | Verified students |

Look at Family for a second. ₦2,500 for six people is about ₦417 each. If you and five other people are each paying ₦1,600 separately, you are collectively burning around ₦7,000 a month for no reason.

## How to pay

1. Open [spotify.com/ng/premium](https://www.spotify.com/ng/premium/) in a browser. Use the browser, not the iOS app, since in-app pricing can differ.
2. Log in.
3. Pick your plan.
4. Choose Credit or Debit Card.
5. Enter your card and your real billing address.

Before you start, know that Spotify prices in naira but is still a foreign merchant, so a naira card can be declined if your bank has international transactions switched off or capped. That is the usual reason a first attempt fails. Banks restricted this between 2022 and 2023 and eased it in 2025.

## The family plan problem

The Family plan is the best value in Nigerian streaming and the most annoying thing to run.

One person pays. Everyone else says they will send their share. Two of them do. By month four you have stopped asking and you are just paying ₦2,500 for six people's music.

## What to do about it

Stop chasing people for their share. Gift it instead.

Subsecute gives you a gift link. Set up the subscription, create the link, and send it to whoever. They can cover a month, or a few months, straight from the link, without needing your card or an account of their own. Someone wants to handle your Spotify for your birthday? They open the link and do it. You want to sort a family member's music without asking anyone for anything? Send them the link.

The money moves as a gift, on purpose, instead of a debt you are quietly carrying every month.

Set. Automate. Relax.
