Claude does not bill in naira. Anthropic charges in US dollars, the same way OpenAI does, and that one fact is behind nearly every problem Nigerians have with the subscription.

The good news is that it is a solvable problem, and the fix is the same one that keeps every other dollar subscription alive.

## What Claude Pro costs

Claude Pro is **$20 a month**, or **$17 a month** if you pay for a year up front. Both prices are in US dollars.

Your bank converts that to naira at its own rate on the day the charge lands, and may add a cross-border fee on top. So the naira figure moves month to month even though the dollar price never changes. Budget slightly above the sticker price, because the rate will not wait for you.

## How to subscribe from Nigeria

1. Log in at [claude.ai](https://claude.ai).
2. Open your account settings and find the plan section.
3. Choose Claude Pro, monthly or annual.
4. Enter your card details, using a billing address that matches the card.

Before you start, confirm your card can actually make international payments. Many Nigerian banks switched international transactions off on naira cards during the 2022 to 2023 forex squeeze and only switched them back on in 2025, usually with a quarterly dollar limit. If the charge keeps failing, that is the first thing to check with your bank, along with what your limit currently is.

## Why the charge fails

It is almost never Anthropic. It is the crossing.

- Your card's international spending limit has to cover $20 at that day's rate, and some banks only re-enabled these transactions in 2025
- If the naira moves, a charge that fit last month might not fit this month
- International transactions have to be enabled on the card at all
- Any conversion fee sits on top of the $20

And nobody warns you. There is no message when a foreign charge is declined. You find out when Claude quietly drops you back to the free plan, usually in the middle of something.

## The fix: fund the card before the date, not on it

The failure mode above has one cause: the money has to be there at the exact moment a foreign company reaches for it, and you have no warning if it is not.

Subsecute inverts that. Claude gets its own virtual USD card, and we charge the card or direct debit you saved a few days **before** the renewal date, convert at the rate shown to you, and put the money on that card. By the time Anthropic asks, the money is already sitting there.

You also get told before any of it happens. A reminder lands on WhatsApp with the amount and the date, and a button to keep it or cancel. Nothing is debited until you say so.

Because it is a card of its own, cancelling Claude never touches your ChatGPT card, and a problem with one merchant cannot reach the rest.

## Paying for someone else's Claude

If you are covering a subscription for somebody else, which is common enough with AI tools in a team or a family, two things are worth knowing:

- Share a gift link and let someone else fund a specific subscription of yours. They pay once at checkout and never see the rest of your account.
- Or run a plan, invite up to 25 people, and set a monthly limit that applies to each of them. Their spending comes out of your balance and stops at the ceiling you set.
