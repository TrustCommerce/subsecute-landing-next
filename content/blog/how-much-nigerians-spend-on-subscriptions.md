Ask someone what they spend on subscriptions and they will guess low. Every time.

Here is a normal setup for someone working in Lagos, nothing extravagant:

| Thing | Per month |
|---|---|
| Netflix Standard | ₦6,500 |
| Spotify Individual | ₦1,600 |
| DSTV or GOtv | Varies by package |
| Cloud storage | ₦1,500 to ₦3,000 |
| One AI tool | Around $20 in dollars |
| Something forgotten | ₦2,000 to ₦5,000 |

That is not a person being reckless. That is a person with a job and a phone.

## The forgotten line is the real problem

Every other line on that list is a decision you made and remember making. The last one is not.

It is the design tool from a project that ended. The storage upgrade from when your phone was full. The trial that converted. Nobody budgets for those, because nobody knows they are there.

Across a year, one forgotten ₦3,000 subscription is ₦36,000. Two is ₦72,000. That is a flight.

## Why it stays hidden

Bank alerts arrive one at a time, on different days, mixed in with everything else you spend. You never see the subscriptions as a group. You see thirty separate debits a month and about eight of them happen to be recurring.

Your brain is not going to assemble that into a total on its own.

## Get your number

Do this once and you will not need to do it again for a while:

1. Open three months of statements.
2. Find every debit that appears in all three months.
3. Add them up.
4. Sit with the number for a minute.

Most people cut something within an hour of seeing it.
