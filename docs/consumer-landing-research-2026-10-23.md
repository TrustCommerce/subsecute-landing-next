Search results are not counted as visual evidence. Static Mobbin screenshots establish composition, not animation timing or conversion performance. ## UI/UX and motion sources

- [NN/g: Homepage design principles](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/homepage-design-principles/): explain purpose early, make the next action clear and avoid motion that obscures the task.
- [NN/g: Scroll-triggered text animations](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/scroll-animations/): waiting for text harms scanning. Keep headings and copy present; animate supporting product objects.
- [NN/g: Animation's purpose](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/animation-purpose-ux/): motion should explain state and relationships. This is established guidance, not a 2026 invention.
- [NN/g: Tabs](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/tabs-used-right/), [GOV.UK tabs](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/components/tabs/): hidden content has a discovery cost; avoid tabs for ordered instructions.
- [web.dev: High-performance animations](https://web.dev/articles/animations-guide): prefer transform and opacity, avoid repeated layout work.
- [Chrome: Scroll-driven animations](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/css-ui/scroll-driven-animations): use native scroll-linked transforms as progressive enhancement, with a complete static fallback.
- [web.dev: View transitions](https://web.dev/learn/css/view-transitions-spas): useful for continuity between views, but unnecessary for this one-page story.
- [WAI: Carousels](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/carousels/): uncontrolled autoplay creates accessibility problems. Use short finite entrances and user-selected examples; honor reduced motion.

## 2026 applicability and 2027 outlook

Current browser capabilities support richer motion without shipping a large animation dependency. That is an implementation opportunity, not evidence that every landing should animate. The inspected examples support variation in composition, real product evidence, strong type and restrained controls. They do not establish a universal style standard or prove better conversion.

For 2027, progressive enhancement, accessible product demonstrations and fast mobile delivery are recommendations. There is no established 2027 convention to cite in October 2026. Avoid chasing glass effects, AI imagery or novelty at the cost of comprehension.

## Design decisions before implementation

1. Lead with the actual home screen, supported by renewal and bill examples, rather than a fabricated calendar as the entire first impression.
2. Show the three setup stages together. Animate the visual journey once on entry, with no scroll trapping or repeated tab bar.
3. Give Expenses a broad editorial layout and retain its actual annual screen. Do not label its annual total as a monthly figure.
4. Center the WhatsApp interaction and use real question-shaped controls. A selection should visibly produce a new reply.
5. Make family payments a photographic section; make gifting a centered product stage with the actual gift-link UI and envelope movement.
6. Keep important prose visible without waiting for animation. All motion has a reduced-motion fallback; no endless autoplay carousel or playback chrome.
7. Retain public navigation, blog, legal links, six visible FAQ answers and matching structured data.

## Copy and product limits

Do not promise that every renewal succeeds, describe closing a card as cancelling a merchant agreement, or imply that Subsecute rewrites a bank statement. Use plain statements about dedicated cards, scheduled funding, reminders and the records in Subsecute. Existing marketing copy is not sufficient evidence for a new backend guarantee. Illustrative amounts are examples, not live prices or exchange quotes.
